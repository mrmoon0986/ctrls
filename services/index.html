The regulators

Financial services under three supervisory regimes

Work where the regulator, the external auditor and the client all end up reading the same working paper, and each of them is entitled to disagree with it. Nothing vague survives that room, so nothing vague gets written.

Regimes
  • UK and Channel Islands: listed banks, fund administrators, trusts
  • Luxembourg: CSSF expectations on ICT and outsourcing
  • EU: operational resilience under DORA
Privacy

GDPR, built before it was enforceable

Framework design and readiness work on GDPR from before it took effect, in regulated financial services and in SaaS companies. Not a gap assessment written against a regulation everyone had already read: the controls, the records and the operating model had to be designed while the answers were still being argued over.

Covers
  • Records of processing and data mapping
  • Lawful basis and consent design
  • Data subject rights as an operating process
  • Cross border transfers and processor controls

What that means for you: when your auditor or your regulator asks a question, I have usually been the person asking it, under the same standard, on a bigger scope. You are not paying me to work out what they want.
